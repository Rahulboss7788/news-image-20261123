VOL. VII — No. 327 MONDAY 23 NOVEMBER 2026 THE WORLD IN ONE PAGE
THE GULF WAR — STRAIT OF HORMUZ

Trump warns of fresh strikes on Iran after 1 January

Tehran says it shot down two US drones; a tanker hits a mine and burns near Oman; Iran offers to reopen the strait — on conditions.

What is claimed, what is known: the drone shootdowns are Iran’s claim; US Central Command denies an American F-16 was hit. The tanker fire and the missing sailor are confirmed by Jordan and maritime reports.

A tanker burns after hitting a mine near Oman’s Khasab coast, as two more tankers are reported hit by projectiles.
SOUTH ASIA — NEW DELHI

Nepal’s new PM meets Modi in Delhi

Balendra Shah, in office since March after his party’s landslide win, holds his first in-person talks with Narendra Modi — energy, hydropower, railways and trade on the table, with new crossings and a rail link due to open.

Shah’s first bilateral trip abroad as prime minister; the two sides are to unveil a joint vision for a “prosperous Nepal, developed India”.
WASHINGTON — PYONGYANG

Kim ignores Trump’s calls and letter, report says

The Wall Street Journal reports Trump tried established phone lines and sent a letter — handed to a diplomat outside North Korea’s UN mission — and got no reply, as the North’s arsenal grows to about 60 warheads.

A renewed push for a meeting during Trump’s Asia trip also failed; Pyongyang set conditions Washington would not accept, the report says.
UKRAINE — KYIV REGION

Kyiv region mourns its dead

Today is a day of mourning for the victims of Sunday’s drone strike on a block of flats in Dymer; three people are confirmed dead and rescuers are still searching the rubble.

THE DAY IN BRIEF

Climate: COP31 ends with no pact, the UN confirms — its chief calls the outcome “deeply disappointing”. Finland: the Russian cargo ship hit on Sunday is still burning in the Gulf of Finland. Economy: India’s oil and gas import bill rose 60% in October. China: Beijing condemns US curbs on its telecom firms.

Every story on this page was first reported on Monday 23 November 2026. Claims are attributed where they could not be independently verified.
